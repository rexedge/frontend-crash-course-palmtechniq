# Assignment 20 — Written Questions

**Type:** Review · **Difficulty:** ⭐ · **Time:** ~15 min
**Prereqs:** all of Class 5
**Answer in:** `work/20-written-questions/answers.md` (or any text file)

---

## Goal

Answer from memory, in your own words, **without scrolling back through the lesson**. If you can't answer one, that's the section to re-read.

---

1. Grid vs Flexbox — one sentence each on when each one shines.

2. What does the `fr` unit mean? How is `1fr 2fr` different from `33% 66%` — especially once there's a `gap`?

3. Explain `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(200px, 1fr))` in plain English, as if to a friend who has never seen CSS.

4. `auto-fit` vs `auto-fill` — what's the difference, and when can you actually see it?

5. Why are grid line numbers a common off-by-one trap? How does `span` avoid it?

6. What are the rules the strings in `grid-template-areas` must follow? What happens if you break one?

7. What exactly does the viewport meta tag do? What does a phone do without it?

8. Why use `rem` instead of `px` for font sizes? And why is `font-size: 5vw` on its own a bad idea?

9. What is "mobile-first"? Why does it use `min-width` queries, and why do they go smallest first?

10. Name three things that make a page responsive *without* any media queries.

---
