# Assignment 14 — Wrap-Up Reflection

**Type:** Review · **Difficulty:** ⭐ · **Time:** ~20 min
**Prereqs:** the whole course
**Answer in:** `work/14-wrap-up-reflection/reflection.md` (or any text file)

---

## Goal

Look back over eight classes and put into words what you learned, what was hard, and what you want next. There are no right answers here. Write honestly, in your own words — a few sentences for each question is plenty.

---

1. Which class's material was hardest for you? How did you get past it?

2. Describe one bug that took you more than 20 minutes. What did you see, what did you try — and what was the actual cause?

3. In your portfolio, where did you use Flexbox, and where did you use Grid? Why each one?

4. What would you add to your portfolio, or change about it, if you had another week?

5. What do you want to learn next: a CSS framework, more JavaScript, a frontend framework, or something else? Why?

---

## Acceptance criteria

- All five answered, in your own words.
- Question 2 names the real cause of the bug, not just the fix.
- Question 3 gives a reason for each choice — "because the lesson did" doesn't count.
- Then test yourself against the lesson's own **Section 14 — Self-Check**, in
  [../class-8-capstone-portfolio-project.md](../class-8-capstone-portfolio-project.md). It covers the whole course.

Keep this file. In six months, when all of this feels easy, it will remind you that it wasn't always.
