# Assignment 24 — Written Questions

**Type:** Review · **Difficulty:** ⭐ · **Time:** ~15 min
**Prereqs:** all of Class 6
**Answer in:** `work/24-written-questions/answers.md` (or any text file)

---

## Goal

Answer from memory, in your own words, **without scrolling back through the lesson**. If you can't answer one, that's the section to re-read.

---

1. `let` vs `const` — when do you use each? What exactly does `const` stop you doing?

2. `==` vs `===` — give an example where they disagree, and say which one you should use.

3. What is the difference between a function that `console.log`s a value and one that `return`s it? Why does it matter?

4. What are the three parts inside a `for` loop's `( … )`, and when does each one run?

5. What is `undefined`, and how is it different from `null`?

6. What does `"3" + 4` produce, and why? What about `"3" * 4`?

7. When would you choose a `while` loop over a `for` loop?

8. What is a parameter, and what is an argument?

9. What index does an array start at? How do you get its last item — and what do you get if you ask for an index that doesn't exist?

10. The Console shows a red error message. What three things does it tell you, and what do you do first?

---
