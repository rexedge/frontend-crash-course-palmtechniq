# Assignment 18 — Written Questions

**Type:** Review · **Difficulty:** ⭐ · **Time:** ~15 min
**Prereqs:** all of Class 4
**Answer in:** `work/18-written-questions/answers.md` (or any text file)

---

## Goal

Answer from memory, in your own words, **without scrolling back through the lesson**. If you can't answer one, that's the section to re-read.

---

1. Which Flexbox properties go on the **container**, and which go on the **items**? List at least two of each.

2. What is the "main axis"? What is the "cross axis"? How does `flex-direction` decide which is which?

3. `justify-content` vs `align-items` — one sentence each, naming the axis each one controls.

4. What does `align-items` default to if you don't set it? Why does that surprise people?

5. When do you need `flex-wrap`? What happens to items that don't fit without it?

6. What does `flex: 1` actually expand to (in terms of grow/shrink/basis), and what does it do in practice?

7. Why is `gap` usually nicer than putting `margin` on flex children?

8. Describe how you'd push exactly one button to the far right of an otherwise left-aligned toolbar.

9. Give one real layout (from this class or your own experience) where Flexbox is clearly the right tool.

10. What does `order` change — and what does it *not* change (hint: think about screen readers and keyboard navigation)?
