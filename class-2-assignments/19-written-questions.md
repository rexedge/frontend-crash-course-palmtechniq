# Assignment 19 — Written Questions

**Type:** Review · **Difficulty:** ⭐ · **Time:** ~20 min
**Prereqs:** all of Class 2
**Answer in:** `work/19-written-questions/answers.md` (or any text file)

---

## Goal

Answer these from memory, in your own words, **without scrolling back through the lesson**. If you can't answer one, that's the section to re-read.

---

1. Name **five** semantic layout elements and say what content belongs in each.

2. What is the quick test for choosing between `<section>` and `<article>`? Give one real example of each.

3. How many `<main>` elements should a page have? Which elements normally sit **outside** `<main>`?

4. Give **two concrete benefits** of semantic HTML over a page built entirely from `<div>`s.

5. What two things happen for the user when you correctly link a `<label>` to an `<input>` with `for`/`id`?

6. When is a `<table>` the right tool? Describe one common **misuse** of tables.

7. Why must all the radio buttons in one group share the same `name` attribute? What goes wrong if they don't?

8. Where does the starting text of a `<textarea>` go — in an attribute, or between the tags? Why is this a common bug?

9. `placeholder` vs `<label>` — what's the difference, and why is a placeholder **not** an acceptable replacement for a label?

10. Name **three** validation attributes and say exactly what each one checks. Which of `type="email"`, `required`, `placeholder`, `minlength` is **not** a validation mechanism?

11. What does it mean that `required` is a "boolean attribute"? What does `required="false"` actually do?

12. What is the difference between `<button type="submit">`, `<button type="button">`, and `<button type="reset">`?

---
